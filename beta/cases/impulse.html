Impulse Spend with more clarity.

A speculative financial assistant connecting a moment of interest on smart glasses with budgeting, saving and deliberate phone approval.

Product conceptAI interaction designFuture of finance
ProjectIndependent product concept
My contributionProduct strategy and experience design
FocusContextual financial guidance
The challenge

What happens between wanting and buying?

My earlier Invisipayment concept explored easier transactions. It also exposed a question: how could a financial assistant help people reflect before spending? Impulse focuses on the gap between noticing something desirable and deciding whether it fits a person’s finances.

My approach

Work back from a preferred financial future.

I used futures thinking and backcasting, starting with a financial assistant that supports wellbeing and working back to an interaction concept. Inspired by the Even G1 smart-glasses ecosystem, the scenario combines an object of interest, retailer information and budget context. It offers a saving or financing path, then moves final authorisation to the companion phone. This is an independent proposal for that ecosystem.

Design decision01

Keep the prompt light. Make approval deliberate.

The glasses can invite someone to consider an option without taking over the view. A financial commitment moves to the phone, where the person can review and authorise it. I paired that separation with visible controls for modes, suggestion frequency and interests, so proactive assistance remains something the person can tune.

Inside the concept

From a moment of interest to a considered choice.

The scenario moves from a quiet wearable view to object recognition, retailer options and a budget-informed saving plan. These are proposed future capabilities.

Explore the interaction

Two paths through the same idea.

The concept films explore saving towards a goal and reviewing a financing option. Both give the person information before a financial commitment.

Designed for personal control

A companion that can be tuned.

Manual, Intelligent and Discovery modes make proactivity a choice. Suggestion frequency, voice notifications and interest priorities let the person tune the assistant’s presence.

A connected service

Map the dependencies behind the promise.

The proposed architecture connects smart glasses, a companion app, bank information and merchant services. It maps how contextual recognition and financial guidance would work together. Hardware sensing, data access and authentication remain assumptions to establish before implementation.
