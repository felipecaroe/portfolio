# ENEM source captures, 5 October 2026

Source: https://apps.staging.luzia.com/enem-prep/?locale=pt

The seven selected PNGs were captured from the actual app at a 390 × 844 CSS-pixel viewport, with a device scale of 3. Files are 1170 × 2532 pixels. They are browser-rendered screenshots, not reconstructed UI.

The outer web toolbar and bottom navigation were temporarily hidden for capture, as requested for the case presentation. Product text, buttons, diagrams, lessons and hint sheets are the real app. Temporary capture styles and viewport settings were cleared afterwards.

The screenshots show selected states, rather than a single continuous exam. The graph-based question and explanation were reopened from existing local study history. A sample question was answered to inspect the feedback flow. No sign-in or real exam submission was involved.

Lower-resolution JPEG review captures are preserved here as references. The beta uses the PNGs. The previous SVG reconstructions were moved to the workspace archive.
