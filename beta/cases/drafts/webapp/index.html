A web assistant designed for browsing, reading and conversation, delivered as working interfaces. Selected surfaces shipped; the rest remain explorations.

Inside the work

Open the chat. Stay in the work.

The sidebar tucks away to give the conversation room. Focus the composer, send a message, then return to Ideas without changing the wider workspace.

The challenge

A larger screen changes the interaction.

The web experience carried phone assumptions into a space with a keyboard, cursor and resizable window. I redesigned the structure for that context: navigation with room to browse, content widths suited to reading, and a composer that belongs to the wider work surface.

My approach

Deliver decisions in working interfaces.

I designed and built the home, conversation and Ideas surfaces against the production reference. The shell collapses its sidebar with the viewport and caps reading width. Shared components carry the product identity across surfaces. Engineers shipped selected interfaces; the remaining designs are documented here as explorations.

Design decision

Give the answer its own work surface.

I removed the assistant’s enclosing bubble, avatar and name label, placing response actions directly beneath the answer. Within the controlled reading width, the content gets the available space. The same shell supports discovery and conversation, with generative result blocks adapted to the wider layout.
