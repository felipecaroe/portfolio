Give a mobile-first assistant a proper place on the web.

Product design delivered as working interfaces: collapsible navigation, readable responses and shared discovery surfaces. Parts of the pass reached production.

Inside the work

Built for browsing and getting things done.

Ideas uses the wider canvas for visual discovery. Tools makes capabilities browsable within the same navigation and content structure.

The challenge

A larger screen changes the interaction.

The web experience carried phone assumptions into a space with a keyboard, cursor and resizable window. I redesigned the structure for that context: navigation with room to browse, content widths suited to reading, and a composer that belongs to the wider work surface.

My approach

Deliver decisions in working interfaces.

I designed and built the home, Ideas, Tools and chat surfaces against the production reference. The shell collapses its sidebar with the viewport and caps reading width. Shared components carry the product identity across surfaces. Engineers merged parts of the pass into production; other parts remain design work.

Design decision

Give the answer its own work surface.

I removed the assistant’s enclosing bubble, avatar and name label, placing response actions directly beneath the answer. Within the controlled reading width, the content gets the available space. The same shell supports discovery and tools, with generative result blocks adapted to the wider layout.
