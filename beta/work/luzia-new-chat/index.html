Research signal · Brazil

Why explore voice first?

Voice is already part of everyday communication in Brazil. These public usage signals, alongside Luzia’s internal usage analysis, made press-to-talk and spoken replies worth exploring as a natural way to begin, with captions for sound-off moments.

4×Brazil sends four times as many WhatsApp voice messages as any other countryMark Zuckerberg · reported by Folha · 2024
80%used WhatsApp audio messages; 97% opened the app dailyOpinion Box · 2025 survey
2×weekly ChatGPT voice users in Brazil over one yearOpenAI · August 2026

Luzia internal signal 42% of users who self-declared as under 13 had used audio, according to aggregate internal usage analysis. This informed the exploration; it is not a prototype outcome.

03 / Interaction decisions

Quiet structure.
Useful cues.

Help someone start, then give the space back to the conversation.

01

Keep the next action close.

A short welcome and nearby suggestions show what Luzia can do. Suggestions give way when the person starts typing or opens the keyboard, keeping the composer usable on small screens.

02

Give tools and history a clear home.

Text, voice and add stay in one compact composer. The drawer holds earlier threads and routes to tools, so the opening does not need to compete with a directory of destinations.

03

Show what the assistant is doing.

The later prototype uses the composer gradient to distinguish idle, listening and generating states. Motion explains changes: the drawer pushes the chat surface and visual tools arrive as selectable cards.

Contextual inference · Design exploration

One request. Three useful directions.

A hungry person can cook, order delivery or book a table. The assistant turns an open request into relevant choices, keeping the next action in the conversation.

04 / Design system

One interaction.
Across states.

The specification covers the moments between the finished screens.

01

Use the same structure in both themes.

I mapped colour to shared semantic roles. Light and dark modes retain the same welcome, composer, drawer and tool structure, with colours that adapt to each surface.

02

Design for the keyboard, not just the cover.

Composer height, keyboard space and prompt visibility are part of the interaction specification. The Android-first, small-screen baseline keeps the conversation usable when space is limited.
