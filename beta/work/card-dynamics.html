The challenge

Trust is built one step at a time.

Connecting a bank account asks people to share sensitive information and understand what happens next. Card Dynamics needed a product direction that felt coherent across its own experience and partner-branded flows.

My approach

Keep the task and reassurance together.

I led the design operations and product and brand revamp. Across account connection, account selection and verification, I aligned the screen structure, partner identity and next-step cues so each stage feels part of one flow.
