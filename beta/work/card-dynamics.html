The challenge

The brand changes. The task must stay clear.

A home-loan journey crosses a retailer, a financial service and the customer’s bank. At each handover, people need to recognise who is involved, which account they are connecting and what they are agreeing to. A white-label product has to carry those distinctions across different partner identities.

My approach

Design the flow and the operating foundation.

I led design operations and the product and brand revamp, preparing the experience for white-label use. The screens connect the retailer’s offer, bank account selection and verification through a shared hierarchy. Partner branding changes while the account details, consent and next action remain prominent.

Inside the work

Partner choice

Keep bank selection easy to scan.

A searchable provider list puts familiar banks first, then keeps the account connection and consent action in the same step.

Account-provider selection · Card Dynamics
Design decision01

Keep consent next to the account choice.

The account-selection screen shows masked account details and balances, then places the mandate agreement beside the connection action. I kept the information needed for that decision together. The person can review the account and its terms at the point of commitment.
