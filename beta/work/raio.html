Employee benefits · UX/UI

Benefits made easier to understand and use.

Discovery and product design for employee benefits, pairing a mobile wallet with an HR management platform.

ClientRaiô Benefícios
My contributionDiscovery leadership and product design
FocusBenefits and account information
Across the platform

Personal choice.
A shared system.

Employees see and use their benefits on mobile. HR manages the offering through a desktop dashboard.

Two audiences, one product

Different tasks.
Consistent structure.

Employees need category balances, transactions and payment actions. HR needs an overview of people and benefits activity. I designed distinct mobile and desktop hierarchies for those tasks, within a shared product identity.

MealsCultureWellness
Design judgement

I gave each benefit category a separate balance card, keeping the total balance and everyday actions in the same overview. The design preserves distinctions between benefits while making the whole account easy to scan.

The challenge

Fragmented administration. Limited employee choice.

The original discovery examined a rigid benefits model: HR managed fragmented platforms, while employees received an offering with limited flexibility. Interviews with employees and business stakeholders informed a product for both sides of that relationship.

My contribution

Design for the person using it and the team managing it.

I led discovery and product design from the research plan through experience direction. The mobile app brings balances, category choices and account actions together; the desktop dashboard gives businesses an administrative overview. The work connects flexible employee benefits with the operational tools needed to offer them.
