The challenge

Many fan needs. One mobile entry point.

The official app spans tickets, food, merchandise, club information and payment. The design challenge was to make those match-day and club tasks easy to find without sending fans through disconnected destinations.

My approach

Keep related actions together.

I shaped a clearer service hub for practical tasks, with member-card benefits and payment connected to the same experience. Common actions are easier to scan, and related steps stay close together.
