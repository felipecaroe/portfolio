A super app for everyday mobility.

Product design connecting station discovery, route planning, payments and rewards across Ipiranga’s services.

ClientIpiranga
My contributionProduct design
FocusMobile super app experience
Connected services

From the journey
to the everyday.

The route view helps plan a stop; the store connects that stop with products and rewards.

Design judgement

I used the journey as the organising thread: find a station, plan a stop, then access payments, convenience and rewards. This gives the wider service offer a practical context. Each capability supports a task the person already understands.

The challenge

The services existed. Their connection was unclear.

Discovery combined customer research, stakeholder interviews and first-hand observation across Ipiranga’s previously separate service areas. The original study surfaced gaps in awareness of app benefits and in recognition of the brand across its touchpoints. The super app needed to make those relationships visible.

My contribution

Make the ecosystem useful at the next stop.

I brought station discovery, route planning, account actions and convenience shopping into a common mobile structure. Points and benefits sit within everyday use, making the relationship between the services tangible in the interface. The route and store screens show that structure at two different moments in the journey.
