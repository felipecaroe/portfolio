Selected work / SkateKing
Community app · Sport

Find your next spot. Then go skate.

A skate community app connecting personal onboarding with map-based discovery of spots, parks and shops.

ClientSkateKing
My contributionProduct and UX design
FocusSkate spot and skatepark discovery
Inside the experience

A little identity.
A lot of local discovery.

Figma screens show the onboarding, then the map, search and spot detail that turn discovery into a real destination.

Design judgement

Skating style is a recognisable choice: street, park, bowl, vertical, freestyle or downhill. I kept it as a short selection with a visible skip option. People can express their identity while retaining a direct route through onboarding.

The challenge

A place to skate is a specific kind of discovery.

A skateboarder is looking for a suitable spot, park or shop in a real location. The interface needs to make those places discoverable while feeling familiar to the community it serves. That led to a map-led product with personal onboarding and a visual language rooted in skating.

My contribution

Connect personal identity with local discovery.

I designed account creation, avatar and skating-style selection, then the map and spot-detail experience. Large choices and clear next actions keep onboarding legible; skate illustrations give the product its character. The map anchors the experience in somewhere the person can actually go.
