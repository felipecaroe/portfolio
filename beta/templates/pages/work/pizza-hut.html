Service design · Food and delivery

Easing the wait between order and arrival.

A service design programme connecting checkout and delivery communication across Pizza Hut’s Brazilian digital channels.

62% → 40%reported checkout-friction score; satisfaction rose from 82% to 95%
ClientPizza Hut · Brazil
My contributionService design leadership for an eight-person team
FocusDigital ecosystem, delivery and checkout
The challenge

Delivery anxiety was a system problem.

Customers moved across more than 25 digital channels before and after ordering. The audit surfaced 65 experience gaps, including unclear progress, inconsistent information and uncertainty about when food would arrive.

My approach

Prioritise the moments that change confidence.

I led an eight-person team through the audit and service design work. We assessed the gaps through usability heuristics, organised them into a prioritised service roadmap and focused the product direction on checkout, order progress and delivery communication.

Inside the work

A connected experience from browse to delivery.

The original service study and product prototype connect the ordering interface to the wider delivery experience.

Design decision01

Make the wait part of the designed service.

The audit put delivery anxiety alongside checkout friction. I treated order progress and delivery communication as part of the core journey, giving reassurance after the purchase a place in the roadmap. A completed payment starts the next phase of the customer’s experience.
