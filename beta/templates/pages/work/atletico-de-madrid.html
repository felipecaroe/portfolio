The challenge

Match day brings several jobs at once.

A fan may need a ticket, food, club merchandise or a way to pay. The official app had to accommodate those practical needs alongside club information. The challenge was to make a useful service easy to recognise within a product people also open to follow their team.

My approach

Organise around what the fan needs to do.

I helped redefine the official app’s fan experience through a service hub, connected shopping and member-card benefits. The hub gives practical actions their own place; the shop carries a purchase into checkout and mobile payment. This makes the commercial offer part of the club journey.

Inside the work

Give club services a clear home.

The member hub brings purchases, cards, coupons, food, tickets and the shop into one recognisable place.

Design decision01

Give services a place beyond the feed.

Tickets, food and merchandise are tasks with a clear destination. I grouped them in a service hub and kept card benefits connected to payment. The design gives fans a recognisable route to act, even when they arrive for club news.
