The challenge

The same meal can start in different places.

I interviewed more than 20 customers across Brazil and went through the dining journey myself. I looked at on-premise and off-premise experiences together, identifying recurring needs across ordering, waiting and the meal itself. The app needed to support the chosen way of dining throughout the journey.

My approach

Turn research into recognisable service choices.

I brought delivery, collection, reservations and queueing into the mobile experience as explicit choices. Food discovery stays close to those actions, while the journey accounts for what happens beyond the screen. The work connects the customer’s intent with the restaurant service needed to fulfil it.

Inside the work

Choose how to dine. Then explore the menu.

Service choices sit alongside food discovery, connecting the app to the visit or delivery that follows.

Design decision01

Ask how they want to dine before they commit.

Delivery, collection, reservations and queueing need different next steps. I made them visible service choices on the home screen, alongside the menu. That distinction gives each journey a clear beginning and keeps the interface relevant to the meal the person is planning.
