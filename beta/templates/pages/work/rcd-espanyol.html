Selected work / RCD Espanyol
Sport · Product strategy · Mobile design

A club app built around being a fan.

Connecting matchday, club content and member services in one recognisably Espanyol experience.

Discovery, product strategy and interface design · 4all Europe

ClientRCD Espanyol de Barcelona
My contributionDiscovery, product and design leadership
FocusFan engagement, membership and matchday
01 / The experience

Useful on matchday. Worth opening between matches.

Club content gives fans reasons to return. Tickets, membership and stadium services stay close when there is a task to complete.

The challenge

Many services. One fragmented experience.

The club already offered digital cards, tickets, video, food and merchandise. Fans struggled to discover them, while repeated logins, PIN checks and webviews interrupted basic tasks. Different club departments also had different expectations of what the app should do.

My contribution

Keep the brief, the design and delivery connected.

As Head of UX and Customer Experience at 4all Europe, I led the account and the product work: stakeholder workshops, research, product direction, information architecture and high-fidelity interface design. I also coordinated priorities, partner dependencies and implementation with the development team.

Project at a glance
ResearchIn-person discovery and five fan interviews
Organisation4all Europe, working with club stakeholders

02 / Discovery

Build a shared view before building the app.

I travelled to Barcelona to facilitate discovery with representatives from across the club. A certainties, assumptions and doubts matrix separated what the organisation knew from what we needed to investigate with fans.

Read the workshop post
Repeated friction

Access should not become a task.

Repeated authentication and hidden membership functions made the app harder to use for practical operations.

A balance to protect

Fans want content and services.

The interviews pointed towards a useful mix of match information, video and club news alongside transactions.

Context matters

Matchday changes the priorities.

Food ordering faced visibility, connectivity and price concerns. Making it easier to find was only part of the problem.

Design decision01

Let context set the hierarchy.

I designed home states around the fan’s situation. Matchday makes the fixture and immediate actions prominent; between matches, stories, news and upcoming fixtures carry more of the experience. El meu RCDE keeps personal services together without turning every visit into a sales journey.

03 / Club content and fixtures

Follow the club. Plan the next match.

Full-screen stories give club media space of its own. The calendar brings upcoming fixtures together, with stadium services close to the fan’s next visit.

From design to delivery

A coherent experience needs connected systems.

I coordinated requirements around BeSoccer match and team data, Geocéntrica ticketing, Espanyol Media and club systems. Match-specific links, membership access and authentication dependencies needed to be resolved alongside the interface, so the visual journey could become a workable product.

What the work established

A shared direction for the fan experience.

The project brought departmental priorities into one product direction, grounded in discovery and fan interviews. It defined the home, navigation and personal-service experience, with a visual language adapted to the club’s May 2026 brand direction.

Design judgement

Keep the commercial opportunities visible, preserve the club’s content value and make the next practical action easier to find.
