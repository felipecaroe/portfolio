Inside the work

A question becomes a reason to return.

Practice moves from a real exam question to a contextual hint, a worked explanation and a simple review loop. These selected states are captured from the responsive staging app.

The challenge

The answer ended the visit.

Students already came to Luzia for homework help, then left when the problem was solved. I designed a path from that existing behaviour into preparation for Brazil’s ENEM exam: a short practice checkpoint, a focused lesson and a reason to return to earlier work.

My approach

Design the learning loop and the content behind it.

I led product design, built the content pipeline and presented the resourcing case. Official exam questions are organised by year and subject, with figures checked against the source papers. Curriculum packs pass through teacher review. Study and practice have distinct modes; rules identify topics, while AI explains an answer the student got wrong.

Design decision

Shorten the route to the first question.

After launch, funnel evidence showed where students stopped before answering. I simplified the start to one tap, shortened setup and removed a feature that was blocking early progress. The iteration focused on getting someone into useful practice.
